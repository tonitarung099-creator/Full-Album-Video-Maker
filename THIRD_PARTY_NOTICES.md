# Third-party notices

## FFmpeg

Windows portable builds bundle the GPL variant from BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds because
Full Album Maker uses the libx264 and libx265 encoders. The FFmpeg executable
and its codec libraries are separate third-party software and are not covered by
the MIT license of Full Album Maker.

The portable folder also includes the license/readme files supplied with the
downloaded FFmpeg build when present. Source/build information is available from
the FFmpeg project and BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds.
